# Criteria for Project Selection

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional university student feedback systems suffer from fundamental structural flaws:

1. **Lack of Identity Verification**: Anonymous feedback often degenerates into non-constructive criticism, toxic comments, or review bombing without pedagogical value.
2. **Missing Accountability**: When feedback is completely untraceable, institutions struggle to distinguish genuine student experiences from malicious bots or external actors.
3. **Stale Cumulative Ratings**: Traditional averages do not reflect recent teaching improvements, punishing faculty for courses taught years ago.
4. **One-Way Communication**: Teachers have no mechanism to respond constructively, ask for clarification on lecture pacing, or engage in pedagogical dialogue.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution: Transparent Campus Feedback

Our system introduces a modern, high-integrity architecture:

- **Institutional Domain Authentication**: Only verified `@thapar.edu` accounts can interact with the platform.
- **Transparent Attribution**: Reviews prominently display the student's name, roll number, and batch, establishing accountability while encouraging constructive feedback.
- **Course-Level Dynamic Mapping**: Reviews are tied to courses actively taught to the student's branch/batch.
- **Dual Time-Decay Rating Engine**: Rewards faculty who actively incorporate student suggestions and improve course delivery over time.
- **Faculty Constructive Dialogue**: Teachers can reply directly to feedback threads to foster open, two-way communication.
