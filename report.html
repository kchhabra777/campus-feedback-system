2. End-User (EU) Interactions Breakdown

A. Student Persona (EU-S)

  • Verified Authentication: Strictly requires @thapar.edu email. Automatic keyword parsing (e.g. be23–be30) locks the role to STUDENT.
  • Student Onboarding: Enters Full Name, 10-digit roll number, engineering branch, batch group (3Q11–3Q15, 2Q11–2Q15), and year of study.
  • Faculty Discovery: Two distinct views:
    • Eligible Teachers: Automatically filtered to teachers registered for the student's active batch/branch.
    • All Campus Faculty: University-wide directory with real-time name & department search.
  • Transparent Review Submission: Submits 1–5 star ratings and descriptive feedback. Student identity is transparently visible as Name (RollNumber) and email.
  • Anti-Spam 21-Day Cooldown: Prevents review bombing by restricting students to 1 review per faculty member every 21 days.
  • Community Validation: Casts helpful/unhelpful votes on reviews (strictly 1 vote per review).
  • Peer Discussion & Reply Voting: Participates in threaded discussion under reviews and upvotes/downvotes peer replies.
  • Content Reporting: Flags inappropriate reviews for administrative moderation.

B. Faculty / Teacher Persona (EU-T)

  • Verified Faculty Identification: Non-student @thapar.edu emails are assigned the TEACHER role.
  • Faculty Profile & Course Mapping: Registers full name, department, designation, and assigns courses taught to specific batch groups (e.g. UCS120 to 3Q11).
  • Time-Decayed Performance Analytics:
    • Overall Time-Decayed Rating: Pedagogical decay weighting (\(w_i = \frac{1}{1 + \text{age}/30}\)).
    • Current Rating: Rolling 180-day recent semester rating.
  • Faculty Constructive Response: Faculty members can reply directly to student reviews left on their courses to seek constructive suggestions or clarify lecture concepts.
  • Platform Integrity Guardrails: Faculty accounts are restricted from reviewing other teachers or self-reviewing.

Mathematical Formulation of Dual Ratings:
• Overall Time-Decay Rating: \(R_{\text{overall}} = \frac{\sum (r_i \cdot w_i)}{\sum w_i}\), where weight \(w_i = \frac{1}{1 + \text{ageInDays}/30}\).
• Current Rating: \(R_{\text{recent}} = \frac{\sum (r_j \cdot w_j)}{\sum w_j}\) evaluated strictly for reviews where \(\text{ageInDays} \le 180\).
