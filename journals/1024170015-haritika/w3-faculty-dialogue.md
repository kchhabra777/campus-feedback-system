# Week 3: Faculty Dialogue & Discussion Threads

## 🎯 Objectives
- Build threaded discussion section beneath each student review.
- Enable faculty members to reply constructively to reviews on their profile.

---

## 🛠️ Key Implementation Details

1. **Faculty Response Input**:
   - When a faculty member views a review on their dashboard, they receive a dedicated input box:
     - Placeholder: `"Reply to student (e.g. ask how you can improve lectures, clarify course pacing)..."`
     - Button: `"Reply as Faculty"`
2. **Verified Faculty Badge**:
   - Faculty replies are rendered with gold/crimson highlighting and verified title:
     `👨‍🏫 bv raghav [Professor (Computer Science and Engineering)]`.
